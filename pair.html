SSH key for your LG TV

From LG webOS SSH Doctor, running on your TV.

1. Enter the code on your TV

2. Choose a key

Don't have one, or not sure? Let this page make one. The private half is saved on this device only and never leaves it.

Or use a key you already have

Pick or paste your public key: the file that ends in .pub, like id_ed25519.pub in the .ssh folder of your home folder. Never the file without .pub.

3. Press OK on your TV

Your TV is asking whether to add this key. Check that the code matches, then press OK on the remote.

Waiting for the TV...

Done: the key is on your TV
